It brings perception, task planning, skill execution, runtime state, memory, and robot interfaces into one architecture, so core capabilities can be reused across wheeled, tracked, quadruped, humanoid, and mobile manipulation platforms.

Build around tasks

Reuse across robot models

OriginMind™ in real operations

Two field projects show autonomous inspection in a large pharmaceutical warehouse and a demanding underground mine.

Pharmaceutical warehouse

Multi-zone autonomous inspection in a large warehouse

During an on-site trial, a wheeled robot used OriginMind™ for mapping, multi-zone navigation, inspection-point management, and mission logging.

Challenge
Cover a large, multi-zone facility consistently while keeping a clear record of each mission.
Approach
Integrate autonomous navigation, inspection-point management, and mission logs on a wheeled robot.
Validation
The team completed on-site adaptation and ran the multi-zone navigation, inspection-point, and mission-recording workflow.

Underground mine

Inspection validation in a GNSS-denied mine

In an operating mine roadway, the robot was tested for perception, localization, and mobility under low light, standing water, dust, and GNSS-denied conditions.

Challenge
Limited visibility, difficult ground conditions, and no GNSS increase both operating difficulty and inspection risk.
Approach
Test environmental perception, localization, mobility, and inspection tasks in the actual roadway.
Validation
The trial validated perception, localization, and mobility under the underground conditions tested.

Who we are

OriginMind™ builds on long-term research at the Laboratory for Autonomous Robot Mobility and Manipulation (ARTS Robotics), Shenzhen Technology University, spanning autonomous mobility and manipulation, embodied AI, SLAM and navigation, and robot control.

OriginMind Robotics Intelligence Co., Ltd. turns these capabilities into OriginMind AI OS™ and leads product development, engineering delivery, and business partnerships. The system is continuously validated in real operating environments, including pharmaceutical warehouses and underground mines.
