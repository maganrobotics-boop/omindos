ABOUT ORIGINMIND

About OriginMind

We develop mobile robots, onboard hardware and software for special-purpose applications.

Robots, hardware and OmindOS

OriginMind provides mobile robots, onboard hardware and OmindOS software for robot manufacturers, integrators and industry teams. We develop the hardware, adapt the software and validate the system around the task.

We work with robot manufacturers, integrators and industry teams to connect hardware and software, test the system and assess how it performs in the intended environment.

RESEARCH AND FIELD WORK

ARTS Robotics

Our research and field demonstrations include work with ARTS Robotics, a robotics research laboratory at Shenzhen Technology University focused on autonomous locomotion and manipulation.

Research spans robot mobility and manipulation, embodied intelligence, mapping, navigation and control.
