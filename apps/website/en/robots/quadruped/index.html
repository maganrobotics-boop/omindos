Inspection development

The quadruped inspection robot is at the engineering prototype stage. The capabilities below describe development directions and still require full-system integration and application validation.

Multi-sensor perception

Combine cameras, LiDAR and inertial measurements to study environment mapping, localization and object recognition.

Language and task understanding

Combine spoken or written instructions with visual information to translate inspection requests into navigation goals and task steps.

Movement on uneven terrain

Evaluate coordinated path planning and quadruped control for steps, slopes and uneven ground.

Inspection task planning

Study scheduled, waypoint and route-based tasks together with visible-light, thermal and other inspection data capture.

Remote monitoring and control

Develop a combined view of robot status, site imagery and 3D maps, with operator intervention when needed.

Inspection records

Develop ways to retain site data, review past observations and organize findings that need attention.

Potential applications

Development assessments focus on site access and sensing needs. Application capabilities depend on validation results.

Mine passages

Assess mobility, sensing and communications around uneven ground, confined routes and equipment inspection points.

Industrial facilities

Evaluate route planning, image capture and temperature monitoring around stairs, service corridors and equipment areas.

Joint development

Work with integrators and site teams to define tasks, sensors, payloads and system integration requirements.

OmindOS inspection system

The interface brings together robot status, 3D maps, inspection points and site imagery. Integration with the quadruped platform will be validated as development progresses.
