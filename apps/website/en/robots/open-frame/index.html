Operating guidance

Key checks for installation, commissioning, operation, shutdown and maintenance.

Installation and initial commissioning

With power disconnected, check fasteners, linkage clearance and cable slack, then confirm supply polarity and interface definitions. On first power-up, read system status before enabling motion. With secure support and protective measures in place, verify zero positions, motion directions and stop functions before limited, low-speed testing.

Operation and shutdown

Confirm the initial posture and commands using the instructions for the specific prototype. On level ground with protective measures in place, test straight-line travel, turning and height adjustment individually at limited speed. To shut down, end the motion task and establish stable support, then disable drives, shut down and disconnect power in the specified order. Stopping travel does not disable the drives.

Communications and maintenance

Before software development, confirm the protocol, node IDs, units and limits; for CAN, also confirm the bus type and bit rate. Inspect linkages, tires, wiring and batteries before and after use. Stop testing and retain logs if abnormal oscillation, binding or communication loss occurs.

Use the configuration and instructions for the specific prototype to confirm power requirements, pinouts, motion limits and stop procedures.
