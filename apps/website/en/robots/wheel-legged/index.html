Product features

Wheel drive and leg joints work together to move the robot. The enclosed upper body houses onboard equipment, with sensing and control configurations selected around the task.

Coordinated wheels and legs

The wheels provide ground movement while the leg joints adjust posture, creating a physical platform for coordinated motion research.

Compact enclosed body

Onboard equipment sits within the upper enclosure. Sensor mounting, power and interface requirements can be assessed together during configuration.

Movement demonstrations

The video shows corridor travel, maneuvering and outdoor movement, making the interaction between wheels and legs visible.
