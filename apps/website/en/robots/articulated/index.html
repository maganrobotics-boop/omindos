Application directions

For mechanism analysis, motion control and application research in teaching and development.

Mechanism and gait education

Use the visible structure to explain linkage motion, foot trajectories and support patterns.

Control development

Tune joint angles, motion timing and gait transitions on physical hardware.

Exploration research

Assess gait, sensing and task requirements for confined spaces or unfamiliar terrain. Application performance requires further validation.

Biomimetic spider research

The six-legged spider in the exhibition material uses modular leg linkages. Servo angles are edited through a host computer to generate spider-like gaits. A separate research video shows the mechanism and compares walking with two-foot and three-foot support. Reconfiguration and multimodal movement remain future research directions.
