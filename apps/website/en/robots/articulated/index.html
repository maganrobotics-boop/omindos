Exploration research

Assess gait, sensing and task requirements for confined spaces or unfamiliar terrain. Application performance requires further validation.

Biomimetic spider research

The six-legged biomimetic spider uses modular leg linkages. Servo angles are set through a host computer to generate walking gaits. The research video shows the mechanism and compares walking with two-foot and three-foot support. Reconfiguration and multimodal movement remain future research directions.
