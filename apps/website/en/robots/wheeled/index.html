Product features

The wheeled mobile robot is intended for ground-level tasks in campuses, warehouses and industrial facilities. The chassis, sensors and OmindOS can be configured around inspection points and site workflows.

Voice interaction

Existing demonstrations show command interaction and movement. Supported commands and task actions depend on the project configuration.

Mapping and navigation

Mapping, localization and navigation modules are optional. The inspection video shows robot movement alongside map building and a first-person view.

Inspection sensing

Visible-light cameras, thermal imaging and other sensors can be assessed for the task. The inspection demonstration shows multiple image feeds and waypoint tasks.
