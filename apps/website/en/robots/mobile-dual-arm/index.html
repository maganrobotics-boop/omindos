Mobile dual-arm task simulation

Coordinating mobility and manipulation

Base positioning

Move the base to the target position and align it to establish a stable starting point for dual-arm operation.

Arm coordination

Study coordinated arm movements and object orientation during grasping, supported lifting and handovers.

Whole-robot coordination

Connect mobility and manipulation, with research into task transitions, state feedback and recovery after faults.

Development and configuration

Validation status

Current materials show the robot design and simulated mobile dual-arm tasks. Dimensions, weight, speed, runtime, arm payload, workspace and accuracy have not yet been validated and are not published as product specifications.

Sensors, communications and end effectors must be assessed for the task. Configuration discussions begin with the workpieces, workspace and test conditions.

Applications and validation

Share your workpiece dimensions, working positions, access space and task sequence so we can define tests for mobility, grasping and coordinated manipulation.
