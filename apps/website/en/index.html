01 Robot platforms

Different platforms. A shared focus on inspection.

Q1

Quadruped inspection robot

A four-legged platform being developed for mine inspection, combining cameras, lidar and inertial sensing with research in autonomous navigation.

T1

Tracked inspection robot

Built around a general-purpose tracked chassis, T1 explores terrain adaptation and environmental sensing for mine inspection.

W1

Wheeled inspection robot

A wheeled platform being developed for autonomous navigation and environmental sensing, with site conditions guiding the configuration.

03 About OriginMind

Grounded in field research.

Field experience and laboratory research guide how we develop robots.

From research to robot systems

OriginMind works with ARTS Robotics at Shenzhen Technology University on autonomous navigation, manipulation and embodied intelligence.

The research spans multisensor mapping and localization, motion control, tactile sensing, planning and dual-arm coordination. Algorithms are evaluated through robot experiments and system integration.

The team’s mining inspection robot system received First Prize in the AI+ application track of the 19th Challenge Cup National College Student Academic and Technological Works Competition.
