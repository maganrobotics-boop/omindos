Autonomous robots for the real world.

01 / Research

Connecting perception, mobility and manipulation.

We connect algorithms, robot design and system experiments to study autonomous perception, decision-making and action in real environments.

01Mobility · Manipulation · Interaction

Interactive loco-manipulation

We study coordinated mobility and manipulation, dual-arm collaboration and human–robot interaction, testing perception, planning and control in disassembly, sorting and deformable-object handling.

  • Mobile manipulation
  • Dual-arm coordination
  • Deformable-object manipulation
02Perception · Language · Action

Embodied intelligence

We connect scene perception and natural-language instructions with action through semantic navigation, visual feedback and skill learning.

  • Semantic navigation
  • Skill learning
  • Execution feedback
03Robot design · Motion · Coordination

Humanoid robotics

Using wheel-legged, legged and manipulator platforms, we study terrain perception, dynamic motion and dual-arm manipulation relevant to humanoid robots.

  • Multimodal locomotion
  • Terrain perception
  • Dual-arm manipulation
04Perception · Localization · Planning

Autonomous robotic systems

We combine LiDAR, cameras and inertial sensing for SLAM, 3D mapping, staircase detection, path planning and obstacle avoidance in unfamiliar, unstructured environments.

  • Multisensor fusion
  • SLAM and navigation
  • Terrain modeling

We test research ideas on real robots through algorithm validation, system integration and experimental evaluation.Explore our research (Chinese)

Research platforms

Platforms for robotics education, research and system validation, covering wheel-legged motion, hardware integration and dual-arm manipulation.

WL1-EDU

Wheel-legged robot

An enclosed-body platform for coordinated wheel–leg control and hardware integration experiments.

Explore the platform

PW1-EDU

Parallel-linkage wheel-legged robot

A parallel-link wheel–leg mechanism with an open frame for accessible wiring, equipment mounting and motion-control experiments.

Explore the platform

DA1-EDU

Dual-arm manipulation platform

A platform for vision-guided grasping, dual-arm coordination, motion planning and digital twin experiments.

Explore the platform

These platforms are currently prototypes. Interface documentation and example programs are being prepared. See each platform page for configuration details and the scope of validation.

Manipulation research

Research demonstrations published in 2024.

Coordinated dual-arm disassembly
