Frequently asked questions

Why does a robot need a task-level OS?＋

Navigation or obstacle avoidance can handle one part of a task. Real tasks require several capabilities to work through a sequence. A task-level OS organizes the steps, connects capability calls and follows the results.

How does it connect to existing robot systems?＋

First assess the robot, control system and capability interfaces, then adapt them around the target task. Robot control and individual skills participate through those interfaces.

How do we start?＋

Hardware manufacturers can share their robot platform and interfaces. Application integrators can share task workflows and site requirements. Together we define the capability combination, integration scope and validation goals.
